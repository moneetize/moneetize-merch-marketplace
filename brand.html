Usage Guidelines

Follow these rules to maintain brand consistency across all touchpoints.

✅

Use icon-only for small placements

Product watermarks, favicons, and app icons should use the icon mark without the wordmark.

✅

Use horizontal for navigation

Header bars and sidebars should use icon + wordmark in a horizontal layout.

✅

Use message lockup for hero areas

Landing pages and marketing materials should include icon + wordmark + slogan.

✅

Maintain clear space

Always maintain padding equal to 0.5× the icon width around all logo variants.

❌

Don’t stretch or distort

Never alter the aspect ratio or apply non-uniform scaling to any logo variant.

❌

Don’t use low contrast

Avoid placing colored icons on similarly-colored backgrounds. Use white or black variants instead.
